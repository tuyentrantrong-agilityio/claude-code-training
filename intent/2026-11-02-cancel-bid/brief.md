# Brief: let people take a bid back

Hi team, bidders keep writing to me because they typed the wrong amount or bid on the wrong auction, and answering them eats my day. I want them to be able to cancel one of their own bids themselves. Once a bid is cancelled it should just disappear from their list. The auction should then carry on as if that bid had never been placed, so the price to beat stays fair for everyone else. Fewer support messages is the whole point.
