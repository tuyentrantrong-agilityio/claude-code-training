# Brief: bids must raise properly

Team, people keep topping the best offer by a single euro and it makes our auctions look cheap and slow. I want every new bid to beat the current best by a minimum step, a flat 5 on every auction. When someone offers too little, they should be told clearly what they need to offer instead. The first bid on an auction should stay free as it is today. This should make auctions end at higher prices, which is what our sellers care about.
